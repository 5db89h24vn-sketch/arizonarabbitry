Weights are the American Rabbit Breeders Association's limits for showing, not a promise for a pet. A cross can weigh more or less; each listing gives its own weights and the day they were taken, and its photos show its ears.

Prices are what mine sold or sell for, from my own records. A few were marked down for a reason, and their listing says why.

A short coat needs a brush once a week. A few of my crosses have a longer, woolly coat that needs grooming every day, and their listing says so.

A cross comes with a crossbred's pedigree: its parents and what is on file of their lines. My crosses: Holland Lop crosses, from Cinnamony (Holland Lop) and Frederick (Satin Angora); Netherland Dwarf crosses, from Saffron (Holland Lop cross) and Odyssey (Netherland Dwarf).
