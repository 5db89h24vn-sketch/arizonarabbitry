Not switched on yet

The counter needs a few minutes in Cloudflare, once. Until then every signup still lands in your inbox as before, and the pages show no number.

  1. Open Workers KV, press Create instance, name it arizonarabbits-waitlist, press Create.
  2. Open Workers & Pages, open arizonarabbitry, then Settings, then Bindings, press Add, choose KV namespace, type WAITLIST as the variable name, pick arizonarabbits-waitlist, press Save.
  3. On the same Settings page open Variables and Secrets, press Add, choose type Secret, name it ADMIN_KEY, type a passphrase only you know, press Save.
  4. Open Deployments, press the three dots on the newest one, press Retry deployment. About a minute later this page works.
