Wednesday, September 2, 2026

This week at the rabbitry

The August litter was six days old in the photo that went up this week: one white and five fawn. A white kit among fawns is not a surprise. An all-white rabbit comes from a gene that can hide in coloured parents and only shows when a kit gets a copy from each side, so one only turns up when both parents carry it. Which kind of white this one is, or whether it is a pale coat that will darken, I can't tell yet. The eyes open at around ten days and that usually settles it. It goes in the litters record with a dated photo like everything else about them.

Of the July four, Sundae and Cloud, the two sable point bucks, are sold. Teddy is reserved. Butterscotch, a fawn doe born 28 July, is the one still available, and 22 September is the date I expect her to be ready.

The biggest change is how the site talks about going home. It no longer names a set age. Kits here go home when they're ready, and I decide that kit by kit. The test is the same every time: off milk and eating hay and pellets on its own, eating and drinking well for at least a week, holding or gaining weight when I weigh it, and nothing wrong with it. Some get there at eight weeks and some sooner. Where a listing shows a Ready date, that's the date I expect for that rabbit.

Each rabbit's card now shows how old it is today, worked out fresh every time you look, in place of a countdown. The listing still gives the date I expect, next to the day it was born.

The site shows a sale honestly too. A rabbit that is spoken for says Reserved with the date it happened, and a rabbit that is paid for says Sold with its date. The front page puts whoever is still available at the top, and sold rabbits stay in the litters record for good, so you can always see what came out of a litter and how it turned out.

Every rabbit has its own address now, arizonarabbits.com/r/butterscotch for example. Text it to someone and they see her photo and a line about her before they even tap. The address keeps working after she is sold, so a link you sent a friend last week still opens the right rabbit.

Pair price, said plainly: take two together and the second one is 20% off. That price also holds if you take one and reserve its littermate before the first one goes home. A second rabbit bought later on is a single price. I would rather it be written here than be a surprise at pickup.

One more thing on the litters page. Under the August litter's day-one photo there is now a strip called As they grow. Each frame carries the date and how old they were that day. Tap one to see it full size and step through the rest.

Monday, August 24, 2026

New name, same rabbits

This used to be Arizona Rabbitry. It is Arizona Rabbits now, at arizonarabbits.com. I was out meeting people and nobody could spell the old one. Most folks heard it as "rabbit tree" and then could not find me later. That is a bad problem to have when you sell rabbits. If you knew me as Arizona Rabbitry you are in the right place, the old web address still brings you here, and the old email still reaches me.

Every rabbit photo on this site is one of mine. There were stock photos here at the start and they are gone. It felt wrong selling real rabbits with someone else's pictures, and you cannot judge how I keep them from a photograph taken by a stranger.
