Site news

Every Monday I post a quick rundown of what's changed on the site. Some weeks that's big news, like new rabbits going up. Other weeks it's small fixes you'd probably never notice. If nothing changed at all, I skip the week rather than make something up.

Monday, August 24, 2026

New name, and the site is live

Big week. The two things worth knowing:

I changed the name. This used to be Arizona Rabbitry. It is Arizona Rabbits now, at arizonarabbits.com. I was out meeting people and nobody could spell the old one. Most folks heard it as "rabbit tree" and then couldn't find me later. That is a bad problem to have when you sell rabbits. If you knew me as Arizona Rabbitry you are in the right place, and the old web address still brings you here. It always will. The old email still reaches me too, so if you have written to me before, nothing you have saved has stopped working.

The stock photos are gone. Every rabbit picture on the site is now one of mine. The one on the front page, the one on the About section, and the three lops sitting in the travel carrier are all my own animals. It felt wrong selling real rabbits with someone else's photos.

The rest is smaller stuff you may or may not notice:

  • The contact form works properly now. I tested it end to end and messages come straight to me.
  • Tap a rabbit's picture and you get a bigger view you can flick through. Right now most of the slots say coming soon, because I am still taking the photos. They will fill in over the next week or two.
  • The Available section shows the date I last checked it, so you can tell whether you are looking at something current.
  • This Site news page is new. It is linked at the bottom of every page and from that availability date.
  • The Call and See rabbits buttons at the bottom of your screen now slide in properly instead of half showing at the edge.
  • The little progress bar at the top works in more places, including when you open the site from a link inside Instagram or TikTok.
  • Fixed the TikTok link, which was pointing at the wrong account.
  • Fixed the answer circles on the contact form, which looked like they were all already picked if your phone was in dark mode.

If something looks broken to you, tell me. I would rather hear it than not.
