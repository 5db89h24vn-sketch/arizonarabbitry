Monday, August 24, 2026

New name, same rabbits

This used to be Arizona Rabbitry. It is Arizona Rabbits now, at arizonarabbits.com. I was out meeting people and nobody could spell the old one. Most folks heard it as "rabbit tree" and then could not find me later. That is a bad problem to have when you sell rabbits. If you knew me as Arizona Rabbitry you are in the right place, the old web address still brings you here, and the old email still reaches me.

Every rabbit photo on this site is one of mine. There were stock photos here at the start and they are gone. It felt wrong selling real rabbits with someone else's pictures, and you cannot judge how I keep them from a photograph taken by a stranger.
