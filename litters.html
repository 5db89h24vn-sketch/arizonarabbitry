Seventh litter Born 6 October 2026 Purebred Netherland Dwarfs

Three born

Two of the three did not make it. The first Netherland Dwarf litter here.

Both were peanuts, the breeders' word for a baby that gets the dwarf gene from both parents. One copy of the gene is what makes a Netherland Dwarf so small. A baby with two copies dies within a few days of birth, however it is cared for. When both parents carry the gene, about one baby in four is a peanut, so they are common in Netherland Dwarf litters. The research.

The father is one of her breeder's own rabbits, so the baby's pedigree comes from his bloodlines on both sides. Her breeder hand-picked her for me. He has been breeding for more than 30 years, and both her parents are Grand Champions.

Sixth litter Born 4 October 2026 Purebred Holland Lops

Six born, none raised

None of the six made it. The second purebred Holland Lop litter here.

Two died the day they were born. The other four got separated in the nest overnight and died of the cold on the morning of 7 October. I tried to warm the last two, but it was too late. At three days old they have no fur yet and keep warm by huddling together. This is the first whole litter I've lost. For Tango's next one, I'll build the nest myself and make sure the babies stay together. The research.
