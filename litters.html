Born 27 August 2026

Six born

Taken the day they were born, before anything else. Six of them, in a nest of shavings and the fur their mother pulled to line it.

At six days old, one is white and five are fawn. Coats can still shift a little as they come in, so the honest read on colour is at four weeks, when I name them. The dated photos below are them growing up; tap any of them to see it full size.

As they grow

The first two weeks decide everything, so I don't name kits or take reservations until four weeks, once I can tell you sex and colour honestly. The day reservations open, the waitlist hears first. The born and raised numbers both go here once the raised number is real, same as every litter.

Born 28 July 2026

Six born, four raised

This is the photo from the day they arrived, before their eyes were open and before they had any fur. The fawn rabbit in the frame is from the litter before this one, about a month old here.

Six were born and four made it through the first fortnight. Losing kits that early is ordinary and it is not something I am going to leave out. This page is worth nothing to you if I only print the numbers that flatter me.

The four went up on the site at four weeks old, once I could tell you their sex and colour honestly. Two fawn does and two sable point bucks.
