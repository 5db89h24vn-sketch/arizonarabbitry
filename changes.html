Site changes

Here is every change I make to this site, with a picture of before and after. The newest is first.

See the rabbit as it is today; the price steps; the payment bar

31 October 2026

Site version 2026.10.31a

Ask for new photos, a video call or a visit, under every rabbit's photos.

The site's photos are not always this week's, so every rabbit for sale now says the day of its newest photo and offers three taps: New photos, Video call and Visit. Each writes you a text or an email to send; I answer it myself, so nothing is booked until I call you back.

New photos is a request, answered the next time I'm with the rabbit.

Your name, a phone if you'd rather have them by text, and anything in particular you want to see. Asking holds nothing: someone else can still reserve first.

Every struck price shows its dated steps.

Under the reason for a markdown, the day the rabbit was listed and every price since, so the struck first price is read beside the prices it was actually offered at. Pecan: listed 19 September at $375; $350 from 24 September; $100 from 26 October; $40 from 30 October.

A payment plan shows how much is paid, with a bar.

A rabbit bought on a payment plan says what has been paid of its price, and what is left, with a bar drawn to match: Honey, $150 of $350, $200 to go. Never a payment's day and never a name.

At $50 or under, the request offers Buy alone.

A rabbit priced at or under the $50 deposit is bought outright, and its request now shows that one choice. Pecan's also showed Reserve and the payment plan, which the page meant to hide.

Site version 2026.10.30b

The big button on the front page says the rabbits can go home now.

It used to say See 5 available rabbits. While every rabbit for sale can go home now, it says See 5 rabbits ready now, decided by the same rule as the cards, so it never promises more than they do; the moment one is waiting for its day, it says available again and the cards say which.

Butterscotch stays here as a producer, in Praline's place.

Her card has left the for-sale rail and she counts in nothing for sale. Her record stays, saying Stays here from 30 October, and her one button opens her producer view; Praline's view says she was a producer until the same day.

Her listing says Stays here, with the day.

No price, no Reserve: the badge says Stays here, the record Stays here 30 Oct 2026, and the button says See her as a producer.

The 21 October litter's father is Odyssey.

Odyssey, my Netherland Dwarf, not Root Beer, as the litter's page said from 21 to 30 October; so the eight are Netherland Dwarf crosses.

Pecan is $40, down from the $375 she was first listed at.

Her fur is patchy in places (fur mites or ringworm, not tested; the photos are on her record), so the price is low on purpose: the difference is for the vet. At $40 she is bought outright: one button, Buy, and no $50 deposit on a rabbit under the deposit.

A markdown is always from the first price.

The price struck beside a marked-down rabbit is the one it was first listed at, never the step before, and the reason is always said: an older rabbit says so, a rabbit with an issue shows the issue. Marshmallow's listing now strikes her first $400 beside $150.

The delivery line is one line, like the pedigree line.

Free delivery statewide, and nothing under it: the days and the weekend rule for long drives are in the answer it opens. The two rows at the top now match.
